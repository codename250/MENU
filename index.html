    BBQ & Main Dish Ordering Rules (주문 안내)
  • Minimum Order: All BBQ and Main Dishes must be ordered for a minimum of 2 servings.
  • Charcoal BBQ (Galbi & Ribs): You can mix and match different Charcoal BBQ items (LA Galbi, Beef Ribs, Pork Ribs) to reach the 2-serving minimum.
  • Fresh Pork Belly & Hot Pot: Pork Belly and Hot Pot dishes must be ordered as at least 2 servings of the same item.
  • Combining Different Grill Types: Charcoal BBQ items and Fresh Pork Belly use different grill setups. Combining both requires min. 2 servings per grill type (4+ total).
