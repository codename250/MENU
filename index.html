    BBQ & Main Dish Ordering Rules (주문 안내)
  • Minimum Order: All BBQ and Main Dishes must be ordered for a minimum of 2 servings.
  • Charcoal BBQ: You can mix and match Charcoal BBQ items (LA Galbi, Beef Ribs, Pork Ribs) to reach 2 servings.
  • Fresh Pork Belly & Hot Pot: Must be ordered as at least 2 servings of the same item.
  • Combining Grills: Charcoal BBQ and Pork Belly use different grill plates. Combining requires min. 2 servings per grill type (4+ total).
